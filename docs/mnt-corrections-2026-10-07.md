# MNT product corrections

Source: user-supplied `Changes in Website - MNT.pptx`, nine slides, reviewed and approved for implementation on 7 October 2026. These corrections supersede earlier website copy for the specified fields.

- Fast & Clear: shelf life two years from manufacture; retain one-minute mixing and specify application to the substrates followed by 20-minute clamping.
- Super Strength: two-minute mixing; retain 24-hour clamping.
- Metallic: metal-filled system; only 12 gm pack including FAQ; clamp 30 minutes, handling strength in one hour, full cure in 24 hours.
- Rapid: replace biocompatibility feature with multi-substrate bonding; packs 3, 7, 35, 87.5, 175, 875 gm and 1.75 kg set; apply to one or both surfaces and clamp 30 minutes; pot life at 25°C nine minutes. Setting time remains ten minutes.
- White Paste: temple manufacturing and repairs, multi-substrate bonding; remove pipe-sealing application from product, FAQ and application placement; two-minute mixing.
- Wood: water-resistant bonding; two-minute mixing of equal volumes of A and B.

Updated shared catalogue data, relevant advisor instructions and FAQ content. Public structured data derives from the same product records. Strong & Clear, Total and Uniweld have no specific corrections in the slides and remain unchanged. Total Gem is separate and unchanged.
