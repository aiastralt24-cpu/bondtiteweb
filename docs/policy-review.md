# Website policy review

Implemented 26 September 2026. These locally authored pages describe the current website and are not a legal compliance certification.

## Before public release

The business owner should confirm the precise operating legal entity, responsible privacy contact, applicable retention schedule, hosting/service-provider arrangements and any international processing. The current application has no automatic lead deletion, which is disclosed. Do not replace that disclosure with an unimplemented retention promise.

Review the Terms & Conditions with the organisation's legal team, including jurisdiction and consistency with official sale and warranty terms. No purchase agreement, universal liability waiver or fictional certification has been added.

If analytics, advertising, third-party embeds or optional cookies are introduced, inventory them and implement any required consent controls before loading them. The current application only sets the administrator session cookie following login. Recheck deployed infrastructure for additional cookies and logging.
