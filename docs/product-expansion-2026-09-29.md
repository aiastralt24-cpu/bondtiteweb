# Catalogue expansion, 29 September 2026

Added 15 products to the existing 22-product catalogue using Astral's Bondtite brand listing and individual product pages. Original product images are stored locally. Descriptions are paraphrased; pack sizes and available application instructions follow the individual source pages.

Rubber and industrial categories were added. All entries use the shared product detail template, metadata, schema, related products, sitemap and llms output. No new advisor suitability rules were introduced.

Products without published instructions use a technical-guidance request; mixing ratios and curing times were not invented. Metallic has conflicting setting-time statements on the source page, so its summary avoids a numerical setting claim. The source's application instructions are retained separately.
